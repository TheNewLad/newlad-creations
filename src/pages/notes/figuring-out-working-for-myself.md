I want to be an entrepreneur and be my own boss. I'm still trying to understand what that looks like for me, especially with how much AI is changing things.

I've tried different ideas, from 3D-printed products on Etsy to Fell for Love. They haven't all gone where I hoped. I'm still interested in sharing that part of the journey.

What keeps coming back is that I enjoy creating. Software, automations, 3D modeling, woodworking — the medium changes.

I'd like to meet people I can talk through ideas with, learn from, and build a community around that curiosity.
