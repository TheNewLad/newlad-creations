Project Drift is a word-association game I’m currently testing. The loop is simple: you get two word prompts, respond with the first association that comes to mind, then receive a new word to continue.

I’m curious about what happens when a game gives you a small prompt and lets your mind take it somewhere unexpected. For now, Project Drift is an experiment in quick responses and changing directions. I’m testing the idea and seeing what the game becomes.
