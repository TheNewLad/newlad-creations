# Notion Work Planner

A comprehensive Notion template designed to streamline your work planning and execution. This template combines powerful database functionality with intuitive views to help you maintain focus and deliver results.

## What's Included

- **Project Database** with custom properties for tracking progress
- **Task Views** organized by priority, status, and timeline
- **Focus Dashboard** for daily and weekly planning
- **Delivery Tracking** to monitor completion and outcomes

## Key Features

- **Multiple Views**: Kanban board, calendar, table, and timeline views
- **Smart Filters**: Automatic filtering by priority, deadline, and status
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual indicators for project and task completion
- **Templates**: Pre-built task and project templates for consistency

## Perfect For

- Project managers seeking better organization
- Freelancers managing multiple clients
- Teams looking for collaborative planning
- Individuals wanting structured productivity systems

## Setup Instructions

1. Click "Duplicate in Notion" to add to your workspace
2. Customize properties to match your workflow
3. Set up recurring tasks and project templates
4. Start planning and tracking your work
