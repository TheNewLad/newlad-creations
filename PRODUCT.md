## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

NewLad Creations is Karim's personal brand website and creative outlet. It
introduces who he is, what interests him, and what he likes to create and build.
It gives him a place to share his creations and the journey behind them.

His full name is **Karim Dallas-Newell**, with a hyphen. Use that spelling in
full-name references, metadata, and the footer; casual introductions use Karim.

Success means visitors get to know Karim through his interests, work, and process.
The site should help him share more openly, meet people who relate to his story,
exchange ideas, and learn from others. Building a community is an aspiration;
there is no confirmed community platform or existing membership to advertise.

## Users

People who connect with Karim's story, curiosity, or work, including other
creators and people who enjoy discussing ideas. No narrower demographic or
professional niche has been selected.

## Positioning

Karim enjoys engineering solutions across software, home automation, physical
making, and media. The connective thread is curiosity and creating things, with
room to show unfinished experiments and ventures that have not gained traction.

He is a software engineer who started coding in a Java computer science class in
2011. He particularly enjoys frontend development and its immediate feedback.
He grew up in New Jersey and moved to Texas after college.

Entrepreneurship is an ongoing exploration. The site must not imply that he has
already achieved business success or has a formula to sell.

## Operating Context

Karim's interests change, and the site needs to accommodate an evolving body of
work. Short thoughts and updates are valid content on their own.

He has made vlogs, had a YouTube channel since 2006, and made two podcasts. He is
currently exploring TikTok because a short brain dump is easier to turn into a
30–90 second video than a heavily planned long-form piece. No publishing cadence
is promised. Social account links remain unconfirmed. Karim selected email for
contact and supplied `hey@newladcreations.com` for public use.

User-confirmed examples; supporting media and links still need to be supplied or

## Brand Commitments

NewLad combines NEW from Newell with LAD, the reverse of DAL from Dallas.
"Creations" reflects the range of things Karim makes. The existing cube logo
references 3D making and X/Y/Z axes. He is open to a completely new site design;
the origin of the name and logo is meaningful context, not a requirement to turn
every page into a cube or a 3D environment.

The voice should be conversational, curious, candid, and lightly sarcastic.
Karim jokes, gives people grace, and tries not to take himself too seriously. He
is also passionate and emotional; humor should not erase that range or turn into
constant self-deprecation. Preserve the spelling "Karim."

Karim described a preference for white, black, natural wood tones such as cedar,
and plant green, associated with Scandinavian/boho interiors. He is visually
oriented and finds examples easier to judge than imagining a design from prose.

## Publication Boundaries

Personal health and therapy disclosure remain undecided. Do not promote private
interview material into website copy, imagery prompts, metadata, or public project
stories without Karim choosing to share it. An existing public mention does not
settle how central that subject should be in the redesigned site.

His concerns about criticism and confidence inform a supportive publishing
workflow; they are not automatic homepage copy. A wish to share openly is not
blanket permission to publish every detail from a planning conversation.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors learn about Karim through the things he cares about and makes.
- Give the journey and process a place alongside the resulting creations.
- Allow the site to reflect his evolving interests and different kinds of work.
- Make sharing a short thought useful without requiring a polished case study.
- Invite conversation without manufacturing success, authority, or a community.

The homepage project marks use Home Assistant’s official logo, Fell For Love’s own
logo, and the user-selected serif Y from Project Drift’s `public/brand/y-serif.png`.
